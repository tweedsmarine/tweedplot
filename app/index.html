          On the chart

          Targets

            Boats you want to pick out: friends, your company's vessels, a race fleet. They are ringed in their group's colour and named on the chart, and their tracks can be drawn. Your own boat's MMSI goes in Settings.
              Tracks are what this app has heard over the last 6 hours (from your receiver or online AIS), so they start when TweedPlot does. Click a boat on the chart and press Add to own fleet in its info window to add it from there.
                Positions come from YB's light Expedition file, which holds each boat's latest fix; tracks build up from the fixes this app has downloaded (kept in tweedplot-data\tracking). Races may publish positions with a delay.
                Groups
                Untick a group to hide its marks on the chart. A locked group's marks can't be deleted or moved (on the chart or to another group) until it's unlocked.
                  TimeZero symbols
                  Show draws only the marks with that symbol, to see what it was used for; then pick its type. The meanings are the same in all your libraries.
                    Effort map
                    Where the boat spent its time, from the tracks listed below (this tab's search and filters, and the Whole library ones), counting only time at speeds in the band: fishing, not steaming. Red is where most time was spent.
                    tokn
                    Logged tracks keep a fix every 10 s while moving (sooner in a tack or gybe) and are saved in the library. GPX goes to the library's export folder.
                    Lines are fences the routing won't cross; zones are areas it won't enter (exclusion zones, reserves, farms not in the charts). Untick a barrier to keep it but not use it. Marks with a barrier radius (edit the mark) are kept clear too. Points move like marks: click one, wait for the green ring, drag. Right-click a barrier to delete a point or the barrier. Routing › Limits and barriers › Keep off barriers turns them all on or off for routings.

                    Libraries alongside
                    Another library (a mate's marks, another season's) shown on the chart with yours, read-only, without mixing them in. Its marks have a ring in its colour; click one to see it. Whole library filters (dates, months, trip) apply to them too. Its tracks are drawn when those filters pick 200 or fewer.
                      Trash
                      Deleted marks, routes, tracks, trips, log events, routings and barriers are kept here for 30 days, then deleted for good. Select and press Restore to bring them back. A route brings its marks back with it.

                      Each file brought in, with what it added that is still here. Undo takes those marks, routes and tracks out again, into the Trash (so it can be put back). Marks that were already in the library and only matched stay.

                                Messages
                                  The Instruments view is one screen of single readings from the same list as the groups. Drag a tile to move it (under another tile it lines up with that tile's column), drag its corner to size it, click it to change the reading. Groups below are for the chart views.
                                    A monitor window is another copy of TweedPlot for another screen. It can show any view, and keeps its own colours (day, dusk or night), view and position, so each screen can suit where it is. Alarms show in every window and can be silenced in any; MOB, Drop and the anchor watch work from any window. Monitor windows reopen where you left them when TweedPlot starts.
                                      Drag a panel by its grip (⋮⋮); double-click or double-tap the grip to fold it down to a small title, and again to open it. Tap a reading to change what it shows, add one next to it, resize or remove it. The layout is kept on this PC and shared by all windows; each group has its own position and size in each view. In the list, type a name for each group and tick the views (Navigation, Planning, Instruments) it appears in; a man overboard shows in every view.

                                        Replay a log

                                        Every Signal K session is logged. A replay drives the boat, instruments, AIS targets and alarms as if live; live data waits until it stops. Tracks are not recorded from a replay.
                                        The buttons in the toolbar at the top left: the live data status (green receiving, amber connected but quiet, red not connected), Live ▶ / Paused ❚❚, Follow and Clear track. Pause to plan: right-click the chart to move the boat.
