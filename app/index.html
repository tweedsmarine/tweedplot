          Online AIS shows what volunteer shore stations hear around the area on the chart (a wider area is asked for when zoomed out, up to about 6°). Get a free key at aisstream.io. Not a substitute for a receiver on board: coverage has gaps and delays.

          Collision and distress alarms

          An AIS-SART, MOB device or EPIRB always raises an alarm. CPA alarms work from live data (or a replay).

          Targets

            Boats you want to pick out: friends, your company's vessels, a race fleet. They are ringed in their group's colour and named on the chart, and their tracks can be drawn. Your own boat's MMSI goes in the Boat panel.
              Tracks are what this app has heard over the last 6 hours (from your receiver or online AIS), so they start when RaceNav does. Click a boat in the AIS popup › Add to own fleet to add it from the chart.
                Positions come from YB's light Expedition file, which holds each boat's latest fix; tracks build up from the fixes this app has downloaded (kept in racenav-data\tracking). Races may publish positions with a delay.
                        Lines are fences the routing won't cross; zones are areas it won't enter (exclusion zones, reserves, farms not in the charts). Untick a barrier to keep it but not use it. Marks with a barrier radius (edit the mark) are kept clear too. Points move like marks: click one, wait for the green ring, drag. Right-click a barrier to delete a point or the barrier. Routing › Limits and barriers › Keep off barriers turns them all on or off for routings.
                          Logged tracks keep a fix every 10 s while moving (sooner in a tack or gybe) and are saved in racenav-data\library\tracks. GPX writes to the library's export folder.
                          Safety depth is set in the Chart panel; routing avoids water shallower than it, and charted dangers and marine farms if ticked there.

                            Display colours

                            The ☀ button at the top right steps through them. With dusk and night colours the window's title bar turns dark too; full screen hides it altogether. Turn the screen's own brightness down at night too.

                            Chart features

                            From the LINZ hydrographic layers in the linz folder (any scale band). Click a light, mark or area for its details. Not for navigation.

                            Tides

                            —
                            Click a tide station for the day's curve, at the weather time when planning. Daily ports from LINZ tables; others from LINZ secondary port differences. Not official tide tables.

                            Own boat

                            Heading line (solid) uses boat speed through the water, else SOG; COG line (dashed orange) uses SOG.

                            Damping

                            Averages the Signal K readings over the time chosen, for the boat on the chart, its lines and the instruments. Position is never damped.

                            Signal K

                            —

                            Replay a log

                            Every Signal K session is logged. A replay drives the boat, instruments, AIS targets and alarms as if live; live data waits until it stops. Tracks are not recorded from a replay.
                            The buttons at the top right: the Signal K status (green receiving, amber connected but quiet, red not connected), Live ▶ / Paused ❚❚, Follow and Clear track. Pause to plan: right-click the chart to move the boat.
                            Large readings for a second screen; touch a reading to change it.

                              Alarms

                              Alarms show in a banner at the top of the chart and sound until silenced. AIS collision and distress alarms are set in Fleet › AIS. Alarms work from live data (or a replay), not in planning.
                              Drag a panel by its grip (⋮⋮); double-click or double-tap the grip to fold it down to a small title, and again to open it. Tap a reading to change what it shows, add one next to it, resize or remove it. The layout is kept on this PC, so the chart table and the cockpit screen can differ.
