                                      Data panel

                                      The data panel sits beside the chart (on narrow screens it opens over the chart). HDG, COG, SOG, STW, depth, position and the time are always at its top, and the alerts at its bottom; what is in between has its own layout for each view (Navigation, Planning) and each Use (Cruising, Fishing, Racing). Choose a panel width above or drag its inner edge. O (or the panel button in the toolbar) hides it to a single line under the chart.

                                      Instruments view

                                      The Instruments view is one screen of single readings. Drag a tile to move it (under another tile it lines up with that tile's column), drag its corner to size it, click it to change the reading.
                                        A monitor window is another copy of TweedPlot for another screen. It can show any view, and keeps its own colours (day, dusk or night), view and position, so each screen can suit where it is. Alarms show in every window and can be silenced in any; MOB, Drop and the anchor watch work from any window. Monitor windows reopen where you left them when TweedPlot starts.

                                        History

                                        Right-click (or hold) any reading — TWS, TWD, pressure… — for its history in a card you can move and size anywhere, or choose a plot from the History group of a tile's menu. Pressure shows its three-hour tendency (Falling quickly is 3.6 to 6 hPa in 3 hours). The history is recorded from live data while TweedPlot runs, and kept when it is closed.

                                          Sound

                                          Alarms (red) sound until silenced; warnings (amber) chime. Every alarm shows in the alerts at the bottom of the data panel (on the line under the chart when the panel is hidden) in every window, and can be silenced from any of them. They work from live data (or a replay), not while planning.

                                          NMEA 2000 inputs

                                          Direct NMEA 2000 inputs are not supported. Connect an NMEA 2000 gateway to a Signal K server and configure it there; TweedPlot receives its data through Signal K.

                                          Replay a log

                                          Every Signal K session is logged. A replay drives the boat, instruments, AIS targets and alarms as if live; live data waits until it stops. Tracks are not recorded from a replay.
                                          The buttons in the toolbar: the live data status (green receiving, amber connected but quiet, red not connected), Live ▶ / Paused ❚❚, Follow and Clear track. Pause to plan: right-click the chart to move the boat.

                                          Averages the live readings (Signal K or NMEA) over the time chosen, for the boat on the chart, its lines and the instruments. Position is never damped.
