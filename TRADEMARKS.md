# Name and logo policy

The GPL covers the code, not the name. "TweedPlot", "TweedsMarine" and their logos
(`branding/`, `app/public/logo-sails.svg`, the app icons) belong to Chris Harris / TweedsMarine.

You may:
- build, run and share unmodified TweedPlot, with the name, from this repository or an official release;
- say your product is "based on TweedPlot" or "a fork of TweedPlot", where it is true;
- use the name to refer to the project, for example in a bug report or a review.

You may not, without written permission:
- distribute a modified version under the name TweedPlot, or under a name or logo that could be taken for it;
- use the TweedsMarine or TweedPlot logos or icons in a modified version;
- suggest that a modified version is made, endorsed or supported by TweedsMarine.

A modified version needs its own name, its own icon, and a different application identifier
(`identifier` in `app/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`, now `nz.tweedsmarine.tweedplot`) and publisher.

Official builds are those published on the releases page of this repository or at
https://tweedsmarine.nz. Only official builds carry TweedsMarine's S-63 activation.
